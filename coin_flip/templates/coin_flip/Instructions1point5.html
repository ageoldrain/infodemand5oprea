Welcome to the Study! Please read the following instructions carefully.

The experiment proceeds in rounds. In each round, two coins will be flipped - a fair one (with equal probabilities of landing heads or tails), which we will call coin 1, and a biased one (with a high chance of landing heads), which we will call coin 2. You will not be informed of their outcomes automatically.

Thus, in each round, two coins will be flipped, and you will not know the results.

Each round has two questions. First, you will be given the option to find out the outcome of one of the two coins. The first question will ask you which coin (the fair or the biased) you'd like to reveal. Once you choose the coin, the outcome will be revealed to you on the next page. After that, you will be asked to guess the outcome of one of the coins.

If you correctly guess the outcome of the coin you chose to reveal, you will be paid the expected value of this round regardless of whether you correctly guessed the outcome of the other coin.

That is, if you choose to reveal the fair coin, and you correcty guess its outcome at the next page, you will be paid 0.95 * $1=$0.95.

OIf you choose to reveal the biased coin, and you correcty guess its outcome at the next page, you will be paid 0.5 * $1=$0.5.

You will not be informed of your earnings after each choice. Your earnings will be displayed to you only at the end of the six rounds.

This process will continue for 10 rounds. The experiment will end after you complete all 10 rounds.
